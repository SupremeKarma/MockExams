## Detailed Course Outlines

### Fundamentals of Information Technology (BIT101CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Computer components and history, hardware and storage, software and databases, networks and the internet, and emerging IT trends.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction to Computer**
2. **Basic Computer Organization and Computer Peripherals**
3. **Computer Storage**
4. **Computer Software**
5. **Introduction to Database**
6. **Networks and Internet**
7. **Information Security**
8. **Computer Hardware**
9. **Technological trends in Information Technology**

---

### Mathematics-I (BIT102HS)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Matrix algebra, coordinate systems and geometry, vectors and solid geometry, and applications of differentiation.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Matrix Algebra**
2. **Coordinate Systems**
3. **Elementary Coordinate Geometry**
4. **Vectors and Solid Geometry**
5. **Applications of Differentiation**
6. **Applications of the Definite Integral**
7. **Functions of Several Variables**

---

### Technical Communication (BIT103HS)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Oral presentation skills, intensive and extensive reading, and professional business/technical writing.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Oral Communication**
2. **Reading: Intensive and Extensive**
3. **Writing**

---

### Society and Ethics in IT (BIT104HS)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Sociology fundamentals, social and cultural change, Nepali society, professional ethics in IT, and emotional intelligence.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction**
2. **Social and Cultural Change**
3. **Understanding Development**
4. **Process of Transformation**
5. **Historical Characteristics of Nepali Society and Culture**
6. **Ethical issues in IT**
7. **Introduction to Emotional Intelligence**
8. **Social Management and Responsibility**

---

### Computer Programming in C (BIT105CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Procedural programming fundamentals in C — control flow, arrays, functions, pointers, structures, and file handling.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Problem Solving with Computer**
2. **Elements of C**
3. **Input and Output**
4. **Operators and Expression**
5. **Control Statements**
6. **Arrays**
7. **Functions**
8. **Pointers**
9. **Structure and Union**
10. **Files and File Handling in C**
11. **Introduction to Graphics**

---

### Project-I (BIT106CO)
**Credits**: 2 | **Type**: Project / Practical

Group software project (2-3 students) built in C, covering requirement gathering through implementation and oral defense — 45 lab hours.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Information Gathering & Requirements**
2. **Algorithms & Flowcharts**
3. **Coding & Implementation**
4. **Documentation & Final Presentation**
