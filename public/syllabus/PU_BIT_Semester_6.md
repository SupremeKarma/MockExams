## Detailed Course Outlines

### Artificial Intelligence (BIT351CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

AI foundations from agents and search through knowledge representation, learning, reasoning, expert systems, neural networks, and NLP.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction & Applications of AI**
2. **Agents: PEAS, Rationality & Agent Types**
3. **Uninformed & Informed Search (BFS, DFS, A*, Hill Climbing)**
4. **Adversarial Search & Constraint Satisfaction (Minimax, Alpha-Beta, CSP)**
5. **Knowledge Representation (Logic, Semantic Nets, FOPL)**
6. **Learning Systems (Decision Trees, Reinforcement Learning)**
7. **Reasoning (Monotonic, Bayesian, Case-Based)**
8. **Expert Systems (Inference Engine, Forward/Backward Chaining)**
9. **Artificial Neural Networks (Perceptron, Backpropagation)**
10. **Natural Language Processing**

---

### Management Information System (MIS) (BIT352CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Information systems in global business, IT infrastructure, decision support and executive systems, and the strategic/security role of MIS.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Information Systems in Global Business Today**
2. **Global E-Business & Collaboration**
3. **Information Systems Organization & Strategy (Value Chain)**
4. **IT Infrastructure & Platform Trends**
5. **Business Intelligence Foundations**
6. **Decision Support Systems (DSS) & Executive Information Systems (EIS)**
7. **Business Information Systems (Marketing, Manufacturing, Finance)**
8. **Security of Information Systems**
9. **Enterprise Systems, SCM & CRM**
10. **Strategic Information Systems & SISP**

---

### Data Warehousing and Mining (BIT353CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Data warehouse architecture and OLAP, plus core data mining techniques — association rule mining, classification, and cluster analysis.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction to Data Mining & Data Warehousing**
2. **Data Warehouse & OLAP Technology, KDD**
3. **Mining Association Rules (Apriori, Market Basket Analysis)**
4. **Multidimensional & Multilevel Association Rules**
5. **Classification & Prediction (Decision Trees, Bayesian, k-NN)**
6. **Cluster Analysis (k-Means, k-Medoids, Hierarchical Methods)**

---

### Simulation and Modeling (BIT354CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Simulation concepts and system types, the Monte Carlo method, random number generation and randomness testing, and analyzing simulation output.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Concepts of Simulation (Types, Advantages, Limitations)**
2. **Monte Carlo Method**
3. **Simulation of Continuous Systems (Queuing, Markov Chains)**
4. **Random Numbers: Generation & Testing (Chi-Square, Poker Test)**
5. **Analysis of Simulation Output & Replication of Runs**
6. **Simulation Languages & Discrete/Continuous Modeling**

---

### Software Engineering (BIT355CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

The software engineering lifecycle — process models, project management, requirements, design, testing, and quality metrics.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction to Software Engineering**
2. **Process Models (Waterfall, Prototyping, RAD, Spiral, Agile)**
3. **Software Project Management (4Ps, COCOMO, Risk, Scheduling)**
4. **Software Requirements & Specification**
5. **Software Design (Principles, Architecture Types)**
6. **Software Testing (Black-Box, White-Box, V&V)**
7. **Metrics for Process & Product Quality (ISO 9000)**
8. **SE Trends: Agile, XP, Cloud Computing, SOA**

---

### Project-VI (BIT356CO)
**Credits**: 2 | **Type**: Project / Practical

Group web-based application project (up to 3 students) built with server-side scripting, on a topic related to Artificial Intelligence or Data Mining — 45 lab hours, evaluated across title, mid-term, and pre-final presentations.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Title Presentation**
2. **Mid-Term Presentation**
3. **Server-Side Web Application Development**
4. **AI or Data Mining-Related Project Topic**
5. **Pre-Final Submission & Presentation**
