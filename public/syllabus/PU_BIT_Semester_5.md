## Detailed Course Outlines

### Research Methodology (BIT301HS)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Scientific inquiry, quantitative and qualitative research designs, hypothesis formulation, and technical writing.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Foundations of Scientific Research**
2. **Literature Review & Research Gap**
3. **Research Design & Sampling Strategies**
4. **Hypothesis Formulation & Testing**
5. **Data Analysis & Interpretation**
6. **Report Writing & Publication Ethics**

---

### Computer Graphics (BIT302CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Rasterization algorithms, 2D/3D transformations, clipping, illumination, and shading.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Display Devices & Raster Graphics**
2. **Line & Circle Drawing Algorithms**
3. **2D Transformations & Clipping**
4. **3D Transformations & Projections**
5. **Visible Surface Detection (Z-Buffer)**
6. **Illumination & Shading Models**

---

### Cryptography and Network Security (BIT303CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Classical and modern ciphers, public key cryptosystems, digital signatures, hash functions, and network security protocols.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Security Concepts & Attacks**
2. **Classical Encryption Techniques**
3. **Symmetric Ciphers (DES, AES)**
4. **Public Key Cryptography (RSA, ECC)**
5. **Hash Functions & Digital Signatures**
6. **Network Security Protocols (TLS, IPSec)**
