## Detailed Course Outlines

### Principles of Management and Entrepreneurship in IT (BIT451MS)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Management fundamentals, organization design, entrepreneurship, business planning, and IT product marketing/technology transfer.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction to Management Principles and Functions**
2. **Organization Design and Decentralization**
3. **The Foundation of Entrepreneurship**
4. **Feasibility Analysis and Crafting Winning Business Plans**
5. **Forms of Business Ownership and Franchising**
6. **Building a Powerful Marketing Plan (Guerrilla Marketing)**
7. **Location Selection and Layout Optimization**
8. **E-Commerce and the Entrepreneur**
9. **Entrepreneur of IT (Technology Transfer & Innovation)**

---

### Distributed and Cloud Computing (BIT452CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Core

Distributed systems fundamentals and cloud computing service/deployment models, virtualization, and cloud security.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Distributed Systems Fundamentals (RPC, RMI, Consistency)**
2. **Cloud Computing Service Models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, Serverless)**
3. **Virtualization Technologies (Hypervisors, Containers, K8s)**
4. **Cloud Storage and Big Data Architectures**
5. **Cloud Security, IAM and Shared Responsibility**

---

### Natural Language Processing (Track A) (BIT471CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Elective

Speech and language processing, morphological parsing with FSTs, N-grams, HMM POS tagging, feature unification, WordNet lexical semantics, and discourse pragmatics.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction to NLP (Linguistic Organization, CFG Parsing)**
2. **Morphology & Phonology (Parsing with FSTs, Phonological Rules)**
3. **Pronunciation, Spelling and N-grams (Smoothing, Language Models)**
4. **Syntax: POS Tagging (HMM Tagger, Transformation-based)**
5. **Sentence Level Construction & Unification Semantics**
6. **Lexical Semantics (WordNet, Homonymy, WSD)**
7. **Pragmatics and Discourse Structure**

---

### Supply Chain Analytics (Track A) (BIT472MS)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Elective

Data analytics and machine learning applied to supply chain management — data preparation in Python, Seaborn visualization, customer RFM segmentation, supplier risk, and demand forecasting.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction to Supply Chain Analytics & SMART Goals**
2. **Data-Driven Supply Chains (Python Setup)**
3. **Data Manipulation & Indexing in Python**
4. **Data Visualization (Seaborn, Geospatial Mapping)**
5. **Customer Management (Cohort & RFM Analysis, Clustering)**
6. **Supply Management & Supplier Risk Regression**
7. **Warehouse and Inventory Optimization**
8. **Demand Forecasting (Time Series Methods)**
9. **Logistics Management & Route Optimization**

---

### Big Data (Track B) (BIT478CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Elective

Big data paradigms in business intelligence — MapReduce workflow anatomy, NoSQL databases (HBase, Cassandra, MongoDB), HDFS storage, and Hadoop HiveQL tools.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction to Big Data (Distributed Systems, Trends)**
2. **MapReduce Applications (Workflows, Optimization, Locality)**
3. **Data Management & Taxonomy of NoSQL Implementations**
4. **Fundamentals of Hadoop (HDFS, Streaming, Pipes, I/O)**
5. **Hadoop Tools: HBase, Cassandra, Pig Latin, Hive & HiveQL**

---

### Mobile App Development (Track B) (BIT479CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Elective

Mobile application development on Android — UI layouts, activity lifecycles, intents, SQLite databases, REST API consumption, location services, and Google Play Store deployment.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction to Mobile Devices & Architectures**
2. **Mobile Platforms & Wireless Communication Constraints**
3. **Introduction to Android Platform (ART, Tools, Manifest)**
4. **Android Application Design Essentials (Layouts, Recycler View)**
5. **Writing Basic Applications (Context, Activities, Intents)**
6. **Data Handling in Android (SQLite, Preferences, Content Providers)**
7. **Developing Real-Time Applications (Telephony, RESTful APIs)**
8. **Debugging, Testing & Deployment (Play Store Distribution)**
9. **Recent Concepts: App Monetization, Location Kit, ML Kit**

---

### Incident Response and Management System (Track C) (BIT485CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Elective

Incident command systems (NEOC/DEOC), early warning alert systems, multi-agency response, disaster recovery portals, and cyber threats to critical infrastructure.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Introduction to Incident Response Systems (NEOC/DEOC)**
2. **Functioning of IRS (Chain of Command, Unified Command)**
3. **Resources and Infrastructure Management (Incident Action Plan)**
4. **Incident Decision System and Reporting (Early Warning, Media)**
5. **Disaster Recovery Portals (DRR Portals, Real-Time Data)**
6. **Phases of Disaster Management (Mitigation, Response, Recovery)**
7. **Cyber Threats and Disaster Management (DR Sites, Fake News)**

---

### Climate Change Risk Management (Track C) (BIT486CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Elective

Climate change science, greenhouse warming physics, climate impacts in Nepal, indicators, extreme weather adaptation, ICT for green growth, and CIRA risk analysis.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Overview of Climate Change Science**
2. **Causes of Climate Change (Greenhouse Effect, Earth Radiative Budget)**
3. **Future of Climate Change (GHG Projections, Ocean Acidification)**
4. **Climate Change Impacts (Nepal Sectoral Impacts)**
5. **Climate Change Indicators (Glacial Lake Outbursts, Phenology)**
6. **Climate Change and Extreme Weather Adaptation**
7. **ICT for Climate Change and Green Growth**
8. **Climate Change Impacts and Risk Analysis (CIRA Framework)**

---

### Disaster Governance (Track C) (BIT487CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Elective

Digital governance models for disaster risk reduction — knowledge repositories, Sendai Framework, disaster mitigation planning, and emergency response mobilization.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Overview of Digital Governance in Disaster Management**
2. **Knowledge Management in Digital Governance**
3. **Overview of Disasters & Sendai Framework**
4. **Disaster Governance Effectiveness and SDGs**
5. **Governance in Disaster Mitigation (Infrastructure Planning)**
6. **Governance in Disaster Preparedness (NSDRM Nepal, Evacuation)**
7. **Governance in Disaster Response (Resource Deployment)**
8. **Governance in Disaster Recovery (Critical Infrastructure Restoration)**

---

### Apprentice Project (BIT453CO)
**Credits**: 3 | **Type**: Project / Practical

45-hour capstone project developing a 2-tier/3-tier/n-tier application with client-side and server-side scripting on any RDBMS.

#### Key Syllabus Units:
1. **Title Identification & Proposal Writing (10 Marks)**
2. **Mid-Term Architecture & DB Design Presentation (20 Marks)**
3. **Pre-Final Application Submission & Demo (30 Marks)**
4. **Final Documentation (APA Format: 20 Marks), Presentation (10) & Viva (10)**
