---

**Note on old vs. new course drift**: Semester 6 old-course (Embedded System, Computer Network,
Data Mining, Advance OOP, Research Methodology) is entirely different from the new-course
Semester 6 (AI, MIS, DWDM, Simulation & Modeling, Software Engineering) — confirming the earlier
finding that PU's BIT curriculum changed substantially between the two versions. When tagging a
past paper by year, check the paper itself first (it usually prints "(New Course)"/"(Old Course)"
directly under the subject) rather than assuming by year alone.
