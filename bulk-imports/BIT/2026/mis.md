Q: What does "IT infrastructure" refer to in an organization?
A) Only the company's office building and furniture
B) The shared technology resources (hardware, software, networks) that provide the platform for the organization's information systems
C) Just the accounting department's software
D) The company's marketing strategy
ANSWER: B
EXPLAIN: IT infrastructure is the set of physical devices, software applications, networks, and facilities shared across an organization that provide the platform on which specific information systems are built and run.

Q: What is an Executive Information System (EIS) primarily designed for?
A) Processing routine daily transactions like payroll
B) Giving senior executives quick access to summarized, high-level information for strategic decision-making
C) Managing a company's email server
D) Only tracking inventory levels
ANSWER: B
EXPLAIN: An EIS is a specialized decision-support tool for senior management, providing easy access to summarized internal and external information relevant to strategic organizational goals, without requiring technical expertise to use.

Q: What is the primary function of a Decision Support System (DSS)?
A) To automatically make all business decisions without human input
B) To help managers make semi-structured or unstructured decisions by providing analytical tools, models, and data access
C) To replace the need for any data storage
D) To only handle structured, repetitive transactions
ANSWER: B
EXPLAIN: A DSS supports managers in semi-structured or unstructured decision-making situations by combining data, analytical models, and an interactive interface, rather than fully automating the decision itself.

Q: What does the "value-chain model" help a business analyze?

B) The specific primary and support activities where competitive advantage can be added at each stage of producing and delivering a product or service

Q: Why does an information system become "vulnerable" to security threats?
A) Systems are never vulnerable if password-protected
B) Because interconnected networks, standardized technology, and increased complexity create multiple points where unauthorized access, errors, or attacks can occur
C) Vulnerability only comes from hardware failure
D) Vulnerability is impossible in modern systems
ANSWER: B
EXPLAIN: Systems become vulnerable due to factors like network connectivity (exposing them to outside access), standardized/widely-known technologies, increasing complexity, and human error — each creating potential entry points for security breaches.
