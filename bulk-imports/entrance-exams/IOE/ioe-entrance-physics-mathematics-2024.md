Q: A projectile is fired with an initial velocity v at an angle θ to the horizontal. If the range of the projectile is R and maximum height is H, then the angle of projection θ is given by:
A) tan θ = H / R
B) tan θ = 2H / R
C) tan θ = 4H / R
D) tan θ = H / (4R)
ANSWER: C
EXPLAIN: Maximum height H = (v² sin² θ) / (2g) and horizontal range R = (v² sin 2θ) / g = (2v² sin θ cos θ) / g. Dividing H by R gives H / R = (sin² θ / (2g)) / (2 sin θ cos θ / g) = (tan θ) / 4. Therefore, tan θ = 4H / R.

Q: In a Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM), when the displacement is half the amplitude (x = A/2), the ratio of kinetic energy to potential energy is:
A) 1 : 1
B) 2 : 1
C) 3 : 1
D) 1 : 3
ANSWER: C
EXPLAIN: Potential energy in SHM is PE = (1/2) k x² = (1/2) k (A/2)² = (1/8) k A². Total energy is E = (1/2) k A², so Kinetic Energy KE = E - PE = (1/2 - 1/8) k A² = (3/8) k A². Therefore, the ratio KE : PE = (3/8) / (1/8) = 3 : 1.
