Q: A block of mass m slides down an inclined plane of inclination θ with constant velocity. The coefficient of dynamic friction between the block and the plane is:
A) sin θ
B) cos θ
C) tan θ
D) cot θ
ANSWER: C
EXPLAIN: When moving with constant velocity, acceleration is zero. The component of gravity pulling the block down the incline (mg sin θ) equals the opposing friction force (f = μ N = μ mg cos θ). Setting mg sin θ = μ mg cos θ yields μ = sin θ / cos θ = tan θ.
