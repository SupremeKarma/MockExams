Q: Which one of the following may NOT be true for an onto function f: A → B?
A) n(A) ≥ n(B)
B) f(A) = B
C) No horizontal line cuts its graph at more than one point
D) No vertical line cuts its graph at more than one point
ANSWER: C
EXPLAIN: For a function to be onto (surjective), every element of B must be the image of some element in A — this is unrelated to how a horizontal line intersects the graph, which is instead a test for whether a relation is a one-to-one function. The other three properties can genuinely hold for onto functions.

Q: Why is the velocity of sound in Hydrogen gas faster than in Oxygen gas?
A) Sound is four times faster in O₂ than in H₂
B) Sound is sixteen times faster in H₂ than in O₂
C) Sound is four times faster in Hydrogen than in O₂
D) Sound is sixteen times faster in O₂ than in H₂
ANSWER: C
EXPLAIN: The speed of sound in a gas is inversely proportional to the square root of its density (v ∝ 1/√ρ). Since oxygen's molar mass (and hence density) is about 16 times that of hydrogen, sound travels √16 = 4 times faster in hydrogen than in oxygen.
