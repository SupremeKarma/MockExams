## 4. Laboratory & Practical Guidelines

Students are required to perform hands-on lab experiments matching each unit's syllabus content:

2. Implementation and execution of standard algorithms and models.
3. Verification and test evaluation with documented log outputs.
4. Preparation and submission of an individualized Laboratory Report notebook.
